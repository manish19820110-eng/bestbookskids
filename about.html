Our Story

Why We Built This

As parents and educators ourselves, we spent hours searching for the right books for our children — only to be overwhelmed by choice or disappointed by poor quality recommendations.

So we built BestBooksKids: a thoughtfully curated platform where every book is reviewed with children's age, reading level, and educational value in mind.

Today, we help over 50,000 families across India discover books that children actually enjoy reading.
