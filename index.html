Reading Tips That Work

01
🕐

15 Minutes a Day

A short daily reading session dramatically improves comprehension and vocabulary. Consistency beats duration.

02
🎯

Match Level to Interest

Let children choose topics they love, even if the book is slightly above their level — motivation beats difficulty.

03
🌟

Read Aloud Together

Reading aloud together builds pronunciation, listening skills, and creates shared family memories.

04
📚

Build a Home Library

Children with books at home read 2× more than those without. Even 20 books makes a real difference.
